01 — About

About our school

Debre Markos Secondary School serves students across grades 9–12, preparing them for the Ethiopian General Secondary Education Certificate Examination and for life beyond the classroom. We combine a rigorous academic program with extracurricular activities in sports, clubs, and community service.
